Who I Am

My name is Charles Joshua Chakma, and I was born and raised in Chandraghona, Chittagong, Bangladesh. Growing up in a Christian family in a predominantly Muslim country, I faced many challenges. In Bangladesh, Christians make up only about 0.4% of the population. As a child, I was constantly surrounded by people of other faiths in my schools and neighborhood. These experiences shaped my early identity and understanding of faith.

Although I was raised in a godly household my father a pastor and my mother a devoted woman leader I later realized that, for much of my early life, I was only going through the motions of Christianity. It wasn’t until my late teenage years that I truly understood what it meant to surrender my heart to Christ. That moment became the turning point of my spiritual journey. I have completed my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, which has equipped me with valuable technical skills. However, my deepest passion lies in serving God and sharing His message. My upbringing, spiritual transformation, and the influence of my parents' ministry have inspired me to pursue a life devoted to advancing God’s kingdom.

How I Found Christ

I grew up in a Christian family where faith was lived out in front of me daily. My parents were strong role models, and I followed the Christian traditions they taught me. At first, my devotion to God was sincere, but as I grew older and encountered different people and beliefs, my relationship with God became more about routine and pride. I began to rely on my biblical knowledge, seeking to impress others rather than truly connecting with Christ. In my pride, I thought my faith made me better than others. The turning point came when God opened my eyes to the reality that my faith was missing the depth of love, humility, and genuine devotion that He desires. I was convicted by the truth that I had been living for myself, not for God. In that moment, I realized that my relationship with Christ had been built on self-righteousness rather than a true commitment to follow Him. In 2020, I repented of my pride and surrendered my life to Jesus. I accepted Him not only as my Savior but also as the Lord of my life, and I was baptized as a public declaration of my newfound faith. That moment marked the beginning of a true transformation in my life. Since then, God has continued to shape me, particularly through my involvement with different ministry and my parent's ministry work . Through meditation getting spiritual maturity and Trying to preach and share the gospel. Today, I continue to grow in humility and deepen my relationship with God, seeking to live according to His will. My heart is now set on becoming a spiritual leader who listens, understands, and walks alongside others, sharing the hope and love of Jesus with those around me.

A Burden for Bangladesh

In Bangladesh, where Islam is the majority religion, many people grow up with misunderstandings about Jesus and Christianity. Because of this, meaningful conversations about faith can often feel difficult or distant.

Many of my Muslim friends and neighbours have never had the opportunity to truly understand who Jesus is beyond the misconceptions they have heard.

Reaching People Through Love & Truth

I feel strongly called to reach people with humility, compassion, and truth, not through arguments, but through Christ-like love and genuine understanding.

My desire is to help break down barriers of misunderstanding and create opportunities for people to encounter the true message of Jesus Christ.

Why I Chose to Study Theology in Quezon City, Philippines

My dream of studying theology abroad is rooted in a deep desire to grow closer to God and become better equipped to serve others faithfully.

I have always longed to study in a Christ-centered environment surrounded by believers who are passionate about living out the Word of God. Studying in Quezon City, Philippines, especially at International Graduate School of Leadership, represents an opportunity to step into a new culture, experience authentic Christian community, and grow spiritually in ways that would shape both my character and future ministry.

I believe that being immersed in such an environment will help me better understand Scripture, witness the power of faith in action, and learn from people who have dedicated their lives to discipleship and missions.

This dream is not simply about receiving an education. It is about preparation for the calling God has placed on my life.

I want to learn how to preach the Gospel clearly, disciple others faithfully, counsel those who are hurting, and serve people with humility, wisdom, and compassion.

Most importantly, I hope to return to Bangladesh better equipped to engage with the Muslim community around me, sharing the love of Christ in a respectful, compassionate, and meaningful way.

Studying theology abroad no longer feels like just a personal ambition. It feels like a calling from God. I believe He has already begun opening doors, and I trust Him to continue guiding each step of this journey in faith.
